Memories Lounge Menu

The lounge menu using corporate colors and fonts has been created with an emphasis on the bar assortment and its description: all drinks are arranged in a strict hierarchy of categories and flavors.
The cocktail list is designed to convey the most important concept - all classic drinks are prepared exclusively according to IBA recipes, and the author's drinks are the quintessence of the knowledge and skills of the bar staff.

Bar Magazine

Based on the principle of glossy magazines with issues dedicated to the changing seasons, this material is a description of the inner workings of the bar with a description of the most important processes and interpretation of the concept: "why the bar uses hypoallergenic protein", "how we prepare caviar from syrup" and much more.
The goal is to draw the attention of the guests to the details, as well as to teach them the culture of drinking.

Beijing-Minsk Hotel Menu

Travel Edition is the name of the menu, which is the quintessence of the main meaning of the appearance of hotels: guests from all over the world can stay at the hotel, the bar menu of which consists of drinks produced in different parts of the world, which are also tourists of some kind. The 79 vertical stripes on the menu cover symbolize the serial number of the 60-year cycle in the Chinese calendar. The menu is designed according to the notebook principle, when the next odd page is wider than the previous one, which makes it easy to get to the menu category of interest.

QR-menu for the 5-star hotel

By building on a Node.The JS page of the QR menu, which I later integrated into a website written in PHP, was my goal, not only to give guests a standard opportunity to easily familiarize themselves with the bar's assortment, but also to get basic information about each of the more than 200 drinks, so I added unique information about each of them.
The page supports a dynamic language change function, as well as a color scheme change.
